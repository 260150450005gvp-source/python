Artificial Intelligence is a technology that enables machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.
 It is used in education, healthcare, business, and many other fields.
 Machine Learning is a branch of Artificial Intelligence that enables computers to learn from data and improve their performance without being explicitly programmed. 
 It is used in many fields, such as healthcare, education, banking, agriculture, and business. Machine Learning helps computers recognize patterns, make predictions,
and solve complex problems. For example, it is used in email spam detection, face recognition, product recommendations, and weather forecasting. 
The main types of Machine Learning are supervised learning,
unsupervised learning, and reinforcement learning. 
 Machine Learning is becoming an important technology because it helps automate tasks,
improve decision-making, 
 and make our daily lives easier.
 Nutrition is the process of providing the body with essential nutrients needed for growth, energy, and good health.
  A balanced diet includes carbohydrates, proteins, fats, vitamins, minerals, and water.
   Good nutrition helps strengthen the immune system,
    improve brain function, and maintain a healthy body. Fruits, vegetables, whole grains, milk, pulses, 
    and nuts are important parts of a healthy diet. Poor nutrition can lead to weakness, malnutrition, and various health problems.
     Children need proper nutrition for physical growth and mental development. 
     Pregnant women also require nutritious food for their health and the healthy development of their babies.
